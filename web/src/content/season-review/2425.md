## The year the Premiership came down to seven points

2024/25 was the odd one out: five in the Premiership, seven in the Conference, three up
and one down, because the new joiners all had to start in the Conference. It was also the
closest the Premiership has ever been.

## Salah went third, and it didn't help

M.Salah scored 344 points, more than any player in either season on record. He went
second overall in the Conference to Mike Nelson, and third overall in the Premiership to
Henry Cartmell.

Mike Nelson won the Conference by 75 points. Henry Cartmell finished bottom of a
five-team Premiership and was relegated. Drafting the best footballer in the country
third overall guarantees nothing, and the group has had a full season to think about
that.

The genuine miss was Luc Durandt taking Saka fourth overall for 127 points, though in a
season where Salah returned 344 that is less a mistake than a rounding error.

## A title decided in the last month

Luc Durandt took the lead in [GW7](gw:7) and held it for twenty-four consecutive
gameweeks. By [GW12](gw:12) he was 81 points clear of Joe Payne. He finished third.

The lead changed hands seven times across four managers, and three of those changes came
in the last eight weeks: Joe Payne took it in [GW31](gw:31), Tom Shiel had it for three,
and Joe Payne took it back in [GW35](gw:35) and won by seven points.

Tom Shiel finished second on 1802. He scored 99 in [GW32](gw:32), the highest single
gameweek of the season by anyone in either league, and it was worth nothing at all.
Chris Roper scored 14 in [GW8](gw:8), which was worth about the same.

## The Conference, and three promotions

Mike Nelson trailed Andrew Newbery by 68 points at [GW16](gw:16), took the lead in
[GW22](gw:22) and never gave it back, winning by 75. Luc Baldwin and Andrew Newbery went
up with him — all three moved into the Premiership for 2025/26, and all three were still
there a year later.

The waiver wire did most of the work. Andrew Newbery's opening-week move for Wissa was
worth 178 points across the season, the best single transfer of the year. Peter Vickers
moved Rice out in [GW4](gw:4) and lost 121 by it. Ivan Rutherford was beaten to Cunha in
[GW9](gw:9), who then scored 146 for somebody else.

At the bottom, Alex Clark finished on 1508, 310 behind the champion, having scored 17 in
[GW16](gw:16).

## The leagues, and the man who beat both

The Premiership won this one comfortably: 28 gameweeks to 10, and 45.7 points per manager
per week against the Conference's 43.7. A five-team league of established players against
seven including the new intake — it was never going to be close.

Except that Mike Nelson won the Conference on 1818, and 1818 would have won the
Premiership too. He went up as champion, and in 2025/26 he was relegated from it.
